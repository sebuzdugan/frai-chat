# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Summary Reference

> ISO/IEC 42001 is a paywalled standard. This file contains only a public
> summary for retrieval context. Readers must obtain the official standard from
> ISO for authoritative text: https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html

## Scope
ISO/IEC 42001:2023 specifies requirements and provides guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) within an organisation. It is designed for organisations providing or using AI-based products or services, ensuring responsible development and use of AI systems.

## Structure (high-level, aligned with ISO Management System family)
- Clause 4: Context of the organisation
- Clause 5: Leadership and AI policy
- Clause 6: Planning, AI risks and AI objectives
- Clause 7: Support (resources, competence, awareness, communication, documented information)
- Clause 8: Operation (AI system impact assessment, design, deployment)
- Clause 9: Performance evaluation (monitoring, measurement, internal audit, management review)
- Clause 10: Improvement (non-conformity, corrective action, continual improvement)

## Annex A — Controls
The standard provides a catalogue of reference controls covering, among others:
- AI policies and governance
- Internal organisation and accountability
- Resources (data, tooling, computing)
- AI system impact assessment
- AI system lifecycle (design, development, verification, validation, deployment, operation, monitoring, retirement)
- Data management for AI
- Information for interested parties (transparency)
- Use of AI systems (human oversight, operation, decommissioning)
- Third-party and customer relationships

## Relationship to other frameworks
- Complements the EU AI Act's high-risk obligations (risk management, data governance, documentation).
- Aligns with NIST AI RMF's GOVERN and MAP functions.
- Integrates with ISO/IEC 27001 (information security) and ISO/IEC 23894 (AI risk-management guidance).

# NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 — Summary

## Purpose
The AI RMF is a voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector-specific framework to help organisations manage the risks of AI systems and promote trustworthy and responsible development and use of AI.

## Characteristics of trustworthy AI
1. **Valid and reliable** — demonstrated accuracy, robustness and generalisation.
2. **Safe** — does not endanger human life, health, property or environment.
3. **Secure and resilient** — maintains confidentiality, integrity, availability under attack.
4. **Accountable and transparent** — clear responsibility, auditability, communicable behaviour.
5. **Explainable and interpretable** — reasonable mental models of outputs.
6. **Privacy-enhanced** — protection of autonomy, identity, dignity.
7. **Fair, with harmful bias managed** — mitigates systemic, computational, and human biases.

## Core functions

### GOVERN (GV) — organisational culture of AI risk management
- GV-1: Policies, processes, procedures and practices for AI risk are in place.
- GV-2: Accountability structures are established so that the appropriate teams and individuals are empowered, responsible and trained.
- GV-3: Workforce diversity, equity, inclusion and accessibility processes are prioritised.
- GV-4: Organisational teams are committed to a culture that considers and communicates AI risk.
- GV-5: Processes are in place for robust engagement with relevant AI actors.
- GV-6: Policies and procedures are in place to address AI risks and benefits arising from third-party software and data.

### MAP (MP) — context is recognised and risks are identified
- MP-1: Context is established and understood (deployment setting, stakeholders, intended purpose).
- MP-2: Categorisation of the AI system is performed.
- MP-3: AI capabilities, targeted usage, goals and expected benefits and costs are understood.
- MP-4: Risks and benefits to all relevant AI actors are mapped.
- MP-5: Impacts to individuals, groups, communities, organisations and society are characterised.

### MEASURE (MS) — identified risks are analysed, assessed, benchmarked and monitored
- MS-1: Appropriate methods and metrics are identified and applied.
- MS-2: AI systems are evaluated for trustworthy characteristics.
- MS-3: Mechanisms for tracking identified AI risks over time are in place.
- MS-4: Feedback about the efficacy of measurement is gathered and assessed.

### MANAGE (MG) — risks are prioritised and acted upon
- MG-1: AI risks based on assessments and other analytical output from the MAP and MEASURE functions are prioritised, responded to and managed.
- MG-2: Strategies to maximise AI benefits and minimise negative impacts are planned, prepared, implemented, documented and informed by input from relevant AI actors.
- MG-3: AI risks and benefits from third-party entities are managed.
- MG-4: Risk treatments, including response and recovery, and communication plans for the identified and measured AI risks are documented and monitored regularly.

## Relationship with other frameworks
The AI RMF is compatible with and complements the EU AI Act (high-risk system controls, transparency), ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management systems), the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and the NIST Privacy Framework. Organisations often map the four functions against EU AI Act Articles 9–15 to demonstrate due diligence.
